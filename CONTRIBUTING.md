# Contributing

Bug reports and feature discussions are welcome in the project issues.
Include a small synthetic or public example and the Stele version. Do not
include private source, private identifiers, credentials or private data.

Code and documentation contributions are not currently accepted. The project
reserves commercial distribution rights, and no contributor agreement is in
place. Please discuss a proposed change before sending a patch. A contribution
policy will be published before outside patches are accepted.

Existing contributions and third-party components keep the rights granted by
their applicable licenses. This policy does not relicense earlier work.
